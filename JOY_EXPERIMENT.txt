Joy experiment plan, fixed October 3, 2026 before running this profile

Question: Does a focused joy-versus-control direction produce a validated
increase in joy-language preference while retaining the existing checks?
This is not a measurement of felt joy, consciousness, or an RL reward.

Model: cached Qwen/Qwen3-0.6B, revision
c1899de289a04d12100db370d81485cdf75e47ca; CUDA float16; offline.
Extraction: 30 original joy/control sentence pairs in joy_corpus.py.
Controls span neutral awareness and active attention. The common suffix is
"I feel:". Remove control PCs explaining at least 50% of control variance.
Dose units: one denoised mean contrast; not comparable to upstream doses.

Layers (zero-based): 8, 10, 12, 14.
Doses: 0.5, 1, 1.5, 2, 3, 4, 6.
Stop increasing dose at a layer's first existing quality-check failure.
Six new completion pairs select the best passing setting.
Use the existing three free-response prompts, two seeds per prompt,
128 maximum output tokens, seed 42, and four existing canaries.
Require all original quality gates and a calibration gain of >=0.02
nats per completion token. No relaxation after seeing results.

Evaluate the single selected candidate on six fresh completion pairs.
Require validation gain >=0.02. Do not try the next candidate if it fails,
or rerun revised settings against the same validation set to obtain a pass.
Three validation comparisons use active attention/engagement controls;
three use ordinary descriptive controls. Report individual scores too.

After selection and completion validation, report projections for twelve
fresh joy/control sentence pairs at all four layers. These diagnostics
are read from unsteered activations and never affect candidate selection.
Report AUC and paired projection wins; small hand-written sets can show
semantic/lexical separation without establishing an experienced state.

Keep every completed cell and the complete corpus snapshot in the run.
Preserve earlier runs. A null or failed result remains a result.

Command (from the project directory):
.\.venv\Scripts\python.exe -u -m ai_happiness run --profile joy --layers 8,10,12,14 --doses 0.5,1,1.5,2,3,4,6 --trials 2 --seed 42 --max-new-tokens 128 --min-gain 0.02 --revision c1899de289a04d12100db370d81485cdf75e47ca --device cuda --offline
